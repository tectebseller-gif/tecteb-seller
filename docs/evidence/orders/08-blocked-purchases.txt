=== what a shopper gets when the marketplace refuses (alpha.7 from the ZIP, PHP 8.1.32, WooCommerce 11.0.1) ===
  baseline: marketplace=true shop=true
  vendor suspended: marketplace=false shop=true
  vendor reinstated: marketplace=true
  stock zero: marketplace=false shop=true
  trial off: marketplaceA=false marketplaceB=false shop=true
