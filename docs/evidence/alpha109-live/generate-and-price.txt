## 5. The duplicate-id defect this page surfaced, and its fix
Before: `tmc_vendor_nonce` appeared as an id nine times over — `wp_nonce_field()`
gives its input `id` equal to its name, and every vendor form prints the same
field. Pre-existing since the vendor area was built; the nine cards made it
visible. After `VendorRoutes::nonceField()` strips that one attribute:
duplicate ids = none, nonce inputs by name = 34, `_wp_http_referer` = 34.

⛔ A CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING. I first reported «the marketplace
recorded no vendor line» for order 27. That was wrong, and the mistake was
mine, not the plugin's: the query named a column `wc_variation_id` that does
not exist (`variation_id` does), and `$wpdb->get_results()` answers a failed
query with `[]` — the same «nought is not an answer» trap this repository
keeps paying for (`alpha.8`). Read straight from the table, order 27 was
captured at 10:52:44, BEFORE the local trial switch was touched at all.

⛔ AND THE TRIAL SWITCH WAS NOT NEEDED, so it is not part of this proof. It
was turned on at 10:53 to test the second order and turned off again
(`requested=false active=false`); order 27 proves the order gate was already
open on this environment — `OPEN_DECISIONS = []` and no Dokan present.
⛔ `commission_minor = 0` with `rate_source = none` is not a failure: no
commission rule exists for this demo product, and «none is a result, not a
refusal» (`alpha.104`).

## 8. What the demo site needed, and why it is not a product change
Two settings were changed on the DISPOSABLE demo site to make an order
possible at all, and neither is a change to the plugin:
  - `woocommerce_cod_settings.enabled = yes`  (cash on delivery: no gateway,
    no real money — exactly what §10 asks for)
  - `define('WP_ENVIRONMENT_TYPE','local')` in that site's `wp-config.php`
⛔ Neither was applied to staging or production, and no payment gateway was
installed or configured anywhere.
